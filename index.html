[ FAQ ]

Frequently asked questions

How far ahead should we book?
For live events, 6–8 weeks gives enough runway for musician and crew staffing. Studio sessions can usually move faster — reach out and we'll confirm timing.
Do you provide musicians and crew, or just direction?
Both. Major Sky handles musical direction and can also staff the musicians, vocalists, and technical crew for the full production.
What genres does Major Sky cover?
R&B, hip-hop, soul, gospel, dancehall, pop, and genre fusions — with particular depth in Caribbean rhythm and identity.
Do you work outside Barbados?
Yes — the team has worked across the Caribbean, in Europe, and with studios in the US.
How do we get a quote?
Call or email with the project scope, dates, and any reference material, and we'll follow up with next steps.
